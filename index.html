General Instructions

  1. The total duration of this exam is displayed per section. The clock will be set at the server and the countdown timer at the top right corner will display the remaining time for the current section only.
  2. When the timer reaches zero, the current section will be submitted automatically and you will be moved to the next section. You cannot go back to a submitted section.
  3. The Question Palette on the right displays the status of each question using the color codes shown below.
  4. Click on a question number in the palette to go directly to that question.
  5. Click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
  6. Click Mark for Review & Next to flag a question for later review (also saves any selected answer).
  7. Click Clear Response to deselect a chosen answer.
  8. Each question has four options with a single correct answer. Correct answer: +4 marks. Incorrect answer: −1 mark. Unattempted: 0 marks.
  9. You may submit a section early using Submit Section, or submit the entire exam using Submit Exam in the palette panel.
